Dataset Requirements Justification

For this assignment, we have selected the "Bici. Aparcabicis" dataset from the Madrid Open Data Portal (datos.madrid.es).

Source URL: https://datos.madrid.es/dataset/205099-0-aparca-bicis/information

Mandatory Requirements

  • R1. Smart City Domain: The dataset perfectly fits the Smart City domain, specifically in the areas of urban mobility, sustainable transport, and city infrastructure.
  • R2. Available as a CSV file: The data has been directly downloaded in .csv format from the official portal.
  • R3. Open License: The dataset is published under an open license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), which allows us to share, adapt, and publish the data freely as long as we give appropriate credit to the Madrid City Council.
  • R4. Easily linked with generic real-world entities: The dataset contains location data (street names, districts, neighborhoods, and spatial coordinates). These entities can be easily linked to generic real-world entities in knowledge bases such as Wikidata, GeoNames or OpenStreetMap.

Optional Requirements

  • R5. Documentation exists: The dataset provides official documentation on the portal, including descriptions of what is included (public street parking) and excluded (sports centers, parks), update frequency and metadata tags.
  • R6. Comes from multiple sources: Although we are currently relying on a single data source (Madrid Open Data) for this initial assignment, we plan to combine it with other datasets in the future. For example, crossing these static parking spots with "BiciMAD stations" or bike lane layouts will allow us to build a much more comprehensive mobility application.
