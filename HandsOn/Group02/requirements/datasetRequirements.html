Dataset Requirements

Main Municipal Parks and Gardens of Madrid

Source: Madrid City Council

URL: Main Municipal Parks and Gardens

CSV: Download CSV

✅ R1. Smart City domain

The dataset belongs to the environmental domain of Smart Cities. It contains information about the main municipal parks and gardens in Madrid, including historical, singular and forest parks.

✅ R2. CSV availability

The dataset is available in CSV format and can be downloaded from the Madrid Open Data Portal.

✅ R3. Open license

The dataset is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license. It allows reuse and redistribution, provided that appropriate attribution is given.

✅ R4. Linkability

The dataset contains several properties that can be linked with entities from other datasets. The most relevant ones are NOMBRE, DISTRITO, BARRIO, LATITUD, LONGITUD and CONTENT-URL. The geographical coordinates can be linked with geographic entities from Wikidata, DBpedia, GeoNames or other geographic datasets. The district and neighbourhood names can also be linked with administrative-area resources.

✅ R5. Documentation

Documentation is available in the Madrid Open Data Portal. It includes a PDF document describing the content and structure of the dataset.

⚠️ R6. Multiple data sources — Partially satisfied

The current dataset comes from a single source, the Madrid City Council. Therefore, R6 is only partially satisfied. However, the dataset can be combined with other sources, such as air-quality, public-transport, Wikidata or geographic datasets.
