Application Requirements - Group 13

Application name: Educa Madrid

1. Concept and Target Audience

Educa Madrid is a mobile application designed for families and students searching for the ideal educational center based on their specific needs. The application allows users to discover schools, high schools, and training centers in Madrid by cross-referencing proximity data, public transport options, and accessibility.

2. Functional Requirements, Data Usage, and Functionality

To ensure optimal performance and the effective use of the relevant information provided by the dataset, we will perform an initial data purge of columns that do not provide useful information or that contain a high percentage of null values (such as PLANTA, PUERTA, ESCALERAS, or FAX), and we will exclusively use the core data from the CSV:

Functionality Dataset Columns
Geographic Search and Filtering PK, NOMBRE, LATITUD, LONGITUD, BARRIO, DISTRITO, CODIGO-POSTAL
Advanced Filters (Typology and Services) DESCRIPCION, TIPO, ACCESIBILIDAD, EQUIPAMIENTO
Routing and Urban Logistics TRANSPORTE, CLASE-VIAL, NOMBRE-VIA, NUM
Direct Contact Actions TELEFONO, EMAIL, CONTENT-URL

With this information, users will be able to search for centers that fit their needs according to all the available filters.
