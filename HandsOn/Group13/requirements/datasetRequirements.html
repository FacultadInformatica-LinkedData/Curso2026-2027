Dataset Requirements - Group 13

Selected dataset: Educational Centers in the city of Madrid
Source: Open Data Portal of the Madrid City Council

Dataset Description

List of educational centers of any type in Madrid: associations, training classrooms, schools, adult education centers, special education centers, public, publicly funded private, and private schools, art schools, nursery schools, secondary schools, etc. As of February 2023, more than 2,100 centers are registered.

Requirements Evaluation (R1 - R6)

R1. Data are in one of the selected smart city domains

The dataset falls within the Smart Education and Smart Community Facilities domains, as it provides critical information about the city's educational infrastructure.

R2. Data are available as a CSV file

The data are available in CSV format, among many others, on the website https://datos.madrid.es.

R3. Data have an open license so they can be published

The dataset is distributed under an open Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license. This allows us to use, transform, and publish the data, as long as we attribute it to the Madrid City Council.

R4. Data can be easily linked with generic real-world entities

The dataset contains many entities and properties that can be easily linked with the Web of Data:

  • Districts such as Chamartín or Centro, which can be linked directly to existing resources in Wikidata.
  • Notable educational centers such as CSIC or historic secondary schools that have their own identifiers in Wikidata.
  • Locations: street or avenue names can be cross-referenced with other repositories.
