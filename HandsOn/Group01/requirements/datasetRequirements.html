Justification of the Dataset used

R1 – Satisfied.
The dataset belongs to the tourism domain, which can be considered part of a smart city context.

R2 – Satisfied.
The data are available in CSV format.

R3 – Satisfied.
The dataset is published under a Creative Commons Attribution license, which allows its reuse and publication with proper attribution.

R4 – Satisfied.
The dataset contains real-world entities such as municipalities, streets, postal codes and tourist accommodations. These entities can be linked with external entities from other datasets, for example municipalities or locations represented in geographic datasets such as Wikidata or DBpedia.

R5 – Partially satisfied.
The dataset includes metadata and a data dictionary, although the documentation of some attributes is limited.

R6 – Not directly satisfied.
The dataset mainly comes from a single official source, although it could be combined with other tourism or geographic datasets.
