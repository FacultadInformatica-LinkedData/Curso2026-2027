AireMAD – Interactive air-quality map of Madrid

Hands-on assignment 1 · Application requirements · Group02

1. Purpose and scope

AireMAD is a mobile application that shows, on an interactive map, the air pollution measured at the monitoring locations in Madrid. Users can select a pollutant and a date, see which areas of the city are most affected, open the history of any location, and compare districts.

The application will be built on top of the Linked Data generated in this course from the Madrid open-data air-quality datasets, which may be linked to other datasets to enrich the information shown.

The application is aimed at citizens who want to check the air quality in their neighbourhood before outdoor activities and people with respiratory problems who need to avoid days or areas above recommended thresholds.

2. Application requirements

The application must:

  • Show a map of Madrid with every air-quality monitoring location.
  • Colour each location according to the pollution level of the selected pollutant.
  • Show the details of a location when clicked: name, address, type, value of the day and the evolution over the last 30 days compared with the reference value.
  • Show additional information about the area where the location is and about the pollutant, obtained from linked datasets.
  • Show a ranking of the most and least polluted locations.
  • Show average values per district and allow comparing several locations over a period.
  • Work on mobile devices.

3. User interface mock-ups

3.1 Main Map View

Interactive map of Madrid displaying air-quality monitoring stations colour-coded according to current European AQI levels. Includes a top pollutant filter bar (NO₂, PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, O₃, SO₂), quick date selector, search functionality, and AQI reference legend.

3.2 Station Detail & Linked Data View

Detailed station sheet showing real-time measurements, secondary pollutant levels, health recommendations, a 30-day temporal evolution chart against the EU binding limit (40 µg/m³), and Wikidata semantic enrichment (substance chemical formula/hazards and municipal district statistics).

3.3 Citywide Pollution Rankings

Municipal ranking contrasting the cleanest locations against pollution hotspots across the city, filterable by timeframe (Today, Past week, This month) and categorized by location type.

3.4 Multi-Station Comparison & Analytics

Comparative interface supporting 3 or more stations simultaneously. Features interactive station tags, overlaid time-series curves against the WHO regulatory threshold (40 µg/m³), and an aggregate statistical summary comparing averages, peak values, and station typologies.
